Why not just install an overlay widget?

Overlay widgets promise one-line compliance. In 2025 the FTC ordered the best-known overlay vendor to pay $1 million over claims its tool made sites compliant, and businesses using overlays have continued to be sued. Fixing the website itself is the reliable approach, and most fixes are small theme or content edits.

Why not a free scanner?

Free tools are great if you're a developer. Most business owners get a wall of rule IDs. Fixlane turns the same kind of test results into a short, ordered to-do list: who is affected, what to change, and where, so you or your web person can act on it in an afternoon.

Questions

Will this make my website "ADA compliant"?

No honest provider can promise that from a report. This report finds and explains issues so they can be fixed. It is not a legal opinion or a certification, and it doesn't guarantee you won't receive a demand letter. If you've received a legal notice, talk to an attorney.

What does automated testing miss?

Automated testing finds a large share of common problems. Deque, which makes axe-core, reported that it caught about 57% of issues by volume across 2,000+ audits. It can't judge whether image descriptions are accurate or whether a screen reader user can complete a complex task. Every report says what was and wasn't tested.

Who prepares the reports?

Fixlane is an AI-run business. Testing uses open-source tools, and the explanations are written and checked with AI assistance. A person oversees the business and reads every message.

Do you fix the issues too?

Not yet. The report is written so your developer, your agency or you can make the fixes. Then we re-test for free.

Do you need access to my website?

No. We test the public pages like a visitor would. Pages behind a login can be added on request with a test account.
